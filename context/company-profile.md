## Product: TaskFlow Pro

**TaskFlow Pro** is a cloud-based project management and team collaboration platform designed for remote and hybrid teams.

### Core Features

1. **Project Boards**
   - Kanban-style boards with customizable workflows
   - Sprint planning and backlog management
   - Gantt chart view for timeline tracking

2. **Team Collaboration**
   - Real-time comments and mentions
   - File sharing and version control
   - Integrated video calls (via Zoom integration)

3. **Time Tracking**
   - Automatic time logging
   - Timesheet approvals
   - Billable hours tracking

4. **Reporting & Analytics**
   - Burndown charts
   - Team velocity tracking
   - Custom report builder

5. **Integrations**
   - Slack, Microsoft Teams
   - GitHub, GitLab, Bitbucket
   - Google Drive, Dropbox, OneDrive
   - Salesforce, HubSpot

## Support Challenge

### Before Digital FTE

- **Support Volume**: 500+ tickets/day
- **Response Time**: Average 4 hours (business hours only)
- **Escalation Rate**: 35% to human agents
- **Customer Satisfaction**: 72%
- **Support Team**: 12 full-time agents
- **Operating Hours**: 9 AM - 6 PM PST (Monday-Friday)

### Pain Points

1. **Limited Hours**: No support on weekends or holidays
2. **Long Wait Times**: Peak hours had 8+ hour response times
3. **Repetitive Questions**: 60% of tickets were "how-to" questions
4. **High Costs**: $75,000/year per support agent + benefits
5. **Scaling Issues**: Hiring couldn't keep up with customer growth

### Digital FTE Solution

**Goals**:
- ✅ 24/7 support coverage
- ✅ <30 second response time
- ✅ <20% escalation rate
- ✅ >90% customer satisfaction
- ✅ <$1,000/year operating cost
- ✅ Handle 80% of routine inquiries autonomously

**Expected Impact**:
- Reduce human agent workload by 70%
- Improve customer satisfaction to 90%+
- Enable human agents to focus on complex, high-value issues
- Scale support without proportional cost increase
